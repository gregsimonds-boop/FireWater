The road back

Help, from the first call.

Use these discussion points when speaking with an independent restoration provider.

01 / RESPOND

Describe the damage

Have your ZIP code, property type, and a description of the incident ready.

02 / ASSESS

Request an assessment

Ask how the provider will evaluate the property and explain the proposed scope.

03 / RESTORE

Compare the scope

Review cleanup, repair, pricing, and any separate authorizations before agreeing.

04 / REBUILD

Confirm follow-through

Ask about reconstruction, documentation, warranties, and who handles follow-up.
